Privacy

Version 1.0, effective 22 October 2026. This notice covers this website, mfhtechnologies.co.za. Each product MFH Technologies owns has its own privacy notice; for DalaDish, see daladish.co.za/privacy.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies. When you visit, the server that hosts it records standard technical details in its logs: your device's IP address, the page asked for, the time, and your browser's name. We use these logs only to keep the website running and secure.

When you email us

If you email us, we keep your name, email address and what you wrote so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Who processes it, and where

The website and our email are hosted in South Africa by our domain and hosting provider. We do not sell personal information or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Server logs are kept for as long as the hosting provider keeps them, usually no more than 90 days. Emails are kept for as long as we need them for the conversation and our business records.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email info@mfhtechnologies.co.za; we will reply within 30 days and will not charge you.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za.
