Licensed platform

The Tiekie platform

The software platform behind the Tiekie service: the application, its financial and tax engines and its data models. MFH Technologies licenses the platform and its engines to Tiekie Financial Services, which operates the service.

Product in private testing

DalaDish

A family meal planner for South African households. It plans a week of meals from the household's budget, prices every meal at the supermarket and turns the week into one shopping list.
